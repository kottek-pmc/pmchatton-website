## Context

Libon is Orange's international calling and messaging app (iOS, Android, web). I worked as a freelancer from 2014, then on mission via PALO IT from March 2016.

## Achievements

- Owned payment methods (card, carrier billing, vouchers) and the e-shop: grew e-shop revenue to **40% of total service revenue** within **6 months**
- Product Owner for the customer-facing apps (iOS, Android, web); defined features with Marketing and Design
- Led a remote team of **10** across **3 locations** (France, Estonia, Egypt)
- Started as Delivery Manager for RCS partners within the Orange Group; scaled delivery to Orange France, international subsidiaries and B2B/telco partners
- Set up the delivery process between an Agile vendor and a time-to-market-driven client
- Partnered with Business Development on carrier partnerships; speaker for Libon at **IMS Forum 2014**
