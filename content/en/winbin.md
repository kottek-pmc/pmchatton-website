## Context

WinBin is a recycling service combining a mobile app, connected sensors and containers to reward users for sorting their waste. I co-founded it as a side venture alongside my main roles.

## Achievements

- Co-founded the company; defined the product vision for the app and the user reward system
- Built the backend API (**Django REST API**) for provisioning and rewards
- Selected industrial partners and managed the technical relationship
- Exited in **2025** after the company pivoted to a hardware focus
