## Context

Nuant is a data and analytics platform for institutional investors in digital assets and DeFi. I joined via Kottek, my consultancy, in July 2021 and was hired full-time in June 2023. I built the French subsidiary from scratch and owned product strategy for the platform's three data products.

## Achievements

- Built the French subsidiary from scratch: scaled to **16 people** and a **€2M budget**; recruited and led the engineering team from **1 to 10**
- Pivoted the trading simulator to agentic configuration after early users found setup too complex: activation rose from **10% to 80%**, **150+ users**
- Took the real-time data stream product from 0 to a standalone, self-sustaining revenue line
- Led **SOC2 and ISO 27001** certification; designed the audit trails and governance features that became enterprise sales enablers
- Introduced an agentic development workflow on the simulator front end, cutting delivery time by **3×**
- Owned strategy for **3 data products** (simulator, real-time streams, monitoring dashboards); built Python/SQL analytics used daily by quant teams
- Mentored a tech lead who became CTO of a subsidiary within **6 months**
