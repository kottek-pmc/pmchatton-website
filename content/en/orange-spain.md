## Context

Orange Spain is one of Spain's major telecom operators. This was a mission via Niji that I sourced myself and brought to Sales; I continued as a freelancer from February 2010.

## Achievements

- Led end-to-end validation of **Joyn/RCS-e** on the test and production networks; the Spanish launch won the **Orange Innovation Award 2013** (Networks)
- IMS/SIP lead for RCS-e: validated device and software vendors' implementations down to protocol level and managed vendor follow-up
- Presented RCS-e services for the GSMA at **Mobile World Congress 2011 and 2013**
- Ran the 2G/3G/4G RAN testbed and validated mobile backhaul architectures before production rollout
- Built triple-play testbed test campaigns and best practices
