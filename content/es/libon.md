## Context

Libon was a consumer mobile communication app under Orange Group with millions of users across Europe and Southeast Asia. I owned the payment and e-commerce product vertical — a high-stakes, high-visibility role within a large corporate structure.

## Achievements

- Owned payment features across web, Android, and iOS — grew e-shop revenue to **40% of total service revenue** within **6 months**
- Led **3 Scrum teams** simultaneously (3 devs, 1 designer, 1 QA each) across international locations
- Managed B2B Telco partnerships across **Europe and Southeast Asia** — negotiating integration terms and delivery timelines
- Delivered on web, Android, and iOS in parallel, maintaining feature parity across platforms
- Drove the full product lifecycle: discovery → design → sprint → release → monitoring
